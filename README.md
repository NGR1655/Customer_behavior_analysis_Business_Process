# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
## Business Analysis Documentation Package
### Retail Customer Retention & Personalization Initiative

> A comprehensive Business Analysis case study demonstrating the end-to-end Business Analyst lifecycle using a real-world retail customer shopping dataset. This project focuses on improving customer retention, increasing loyalty program adoption, and enabling personalized customer experiences through structured business analysis and documentation.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This repository contains a complete Business Analysis documentation package developed from the **Customer Shopping Behavior** dataset containing **3,900 retail customer transaction records**.

The project simulates how a Business Analyst would work with stakeholders to identify business problems, gather requirements, analyze existing business processes, and recommend data-driven solutions for a retail organization.

The objective is to improve:

- Customer Retention
- Loyalty Program Adoption
- Personalized Marketing
- Discount Optimization
- Customer Experience
- Business Decision Making

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The retail company has a large and active customer base but lacks an effective strategy for customer retention and personalization.

Key business challenges include:

- Low subscription (loyalty) enrollment
- Inconsistent discount strategy
- Limited personalized customer engagement
- Missed cross-selling opportunities
- No standardized business process for customer retention initiatives

This project documents how these challenges can be addressed using Business Analysis methodologies.

# 📈 Key Business Insights

### Loyalty Program

- Only **27%** of customers are enrolled in the subscription program.
- Significant opportunity exists to increase customer retention through targeted enrollment campaigns.

### Discount Strategy

- **100%** of subscribed customers received discounts.
- Only **28%** of non-subscribed customers received discounts.
- Current discount strategy is reactive rather than data-driven.

### Product Performance

- Clothing accounts for **44.5%** of purchases.
- Accessories contribute **31.8%**.
- Footwear and Outerwear represent strong cross-selling opportunities.

### Customer Satisfaction

- Average Review Rating: **3.75 / 5**
- Average Previous Purchases: **25**

This indicates an engaged customer base with room for improved customer experience and retention.
